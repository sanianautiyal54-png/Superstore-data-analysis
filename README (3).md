# Superstore Data Analysis | Data Analyst Assessment

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a complete data analysis of the **Superstore dataset**, covering the workflow from data preparation to business recommendations.

The analysis was performed using **Excel, SQL Server, Python, and Power BI** to understand sales performance, profitability, customer and product performance, regional trends, discounts, and potential outliers.

The goal was to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Calculate important business KPIs
- Identify high-performing categories and regions
- Analyze customer and product performance
- Identify loss-making products
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profit
- Identify potential outliers
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Provide practical business recommendations
